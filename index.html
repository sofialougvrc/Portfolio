Undergraduate at Sapienza University of Rome, splitting time between two very different worlds: a BSc in Mathematics and AI, and a BA in History, Anthropology, and Religions, focused on Medieval History and Paleography. One half of me is building models and chasing machine learning and quantitative research. The other half is elbow-deep in medieval manuscripts, learning to read and date documents most people would just walk past in a museum case. Somewhere in between, I'm also building an R2-D2 from scratch and a GoTo telescope engine, and I watch horror movies too, if that tells you anything. Say hi by email if any of this overlaps with you.
