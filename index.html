Winamp - Research Player
-0:00
128kbps44kHzstereo
Independent ML research on grokking, where small transformers memorize training data quickly but only generalize much later. I tested whether the signal-to-noise ratio of the gradient norm can warn of this switch in advance. Across 15 runs, the SNR decline preceded the jump in test accuracy every time, by about 2,000 steps on average, and beat a naive training-loss baseline. It needs no extra computation. This is a small study on one task and one model, so it shows a consistent pattern, not a proven mechanism.
